I've always believed that driving isn't just getting from point A to point B. It's a nuanced dance of intuition, awareness, and decision-making. That's why I created the "Driving" repository on GitHub. 

## What It's All About

This repo is all about exploring driving safety and efficiency through a framework I call the "win-set." It’s my personal take on navigating the intricate web of rules, risks, and responsibilities that come with driving. 

Driving isn't just a matter of regulation; it’s about minimizing risks, staying within the law, and ensuring vehicle longevity. My aim is to articulate driving goals that matter and the constraints we're all dealing with. 

### Why I Built It

Driving can be stressful and dangerous, especially when you consider the daily uncertainties we face—other drivers, weather conditions, mechanical failures. This project serves as a space to document my thoughts and strategies for coping with these challenges. I wanted to create a structured approach to driving that emphasizes:

- **Risk Minimization:** How can I avoid dangerous situations?
- **Legal Compliance:** What are the laws and regulations I need to follow?
- **Vehicle Preservation:** How do I keep my car running smoothly?

## Tech Stack

I kept it lightweight, focusing solely on Markdown for documentation. This choice aligns with the project's essence—clarity without distractions. There are no flashy bells and whistles here. Just raw, straightforward content targeting anyone interested in improving their driving strategy.

## Design Decisions and Tradeoffs

When I embarked on this project, I opted for simplicity. Here’s what shaped my choices:

- **Markdown:** It’s accessible. You don't need a complex setup to read or contribute.
- **Focus on Documentation:** While I considered adding fancy tools or applications, I decided to prioritize clear articulation over development complexity.

The tradeoff? It may not be a robust software application yet, but it’s an insightful knowledge base aimed at improving understanding of a vital everyday activity.
